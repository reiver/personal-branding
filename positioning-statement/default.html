Personal Branding Positioning Statement

a declaration that defines who you can help, the specific problem you can solve, and your unique approach

by Charles Iliya Krempeaux

A personal brand positioning statement is a declaration that defines who you can help, the specific problem you can solve, and your unique approach. It helps guide what you do and don't do when you engage in personal branding.

Personal Branding Positioning Statement tend to be punchy and easily digested.

Personal Branding Positioning Statement tend to focus on your target audience's problems, what you can do for them, and perhaps how you can solve them differently — rather than your personal history.

Personal Brand and Personal Branding

You can think of your personal brand as what other people say about you when you aren't there. Personal Branding is an effort to try to influence what those other people say about you.

Whether you engage in personal branding or not, your have a personal brand. Your personal brand is sort of like your reputation with a recognizable theme. Your personal brand is your reputation, your work ethic, how you treat others, what other people think when they hear you name, who you are, what you are good at, what you stand for, how you consisently show it, etc. Personal Branding is an effort affect that narrative told about you.

For example, if someone asks, “Who should I ask about photography?”, and a particular person's name immediately comes to your mind, that person has developed a strong association — i.e., a personal brand — around photography.

So, everyone has a personal brand whether they engage in personal branding or not. And, personal branding means deliberately trying to shape what you want people to remember about you. Which may involve consistently backing-up what you want other people to remember about you with your actions.

Positioning Statement Template

You want to be able to eventually write a positioning statement about yourself of the form:

I help SPECIFIC_AUDIENCE to ACHIEVE_SPECIFIC_OUTCOME through YOUR_DISTINCTIVE_EXPERTISE_OR_APPROACH_OR_METHOD.

Where “SPECIFIC_AUDIENCE”, “ACHIEVE_SPECIFIC_OUTCOME”, and “YOUR_DISTINCTIVE_EXPERTISE_OR_APPROACH_OR_METHOD” will be replaced (in your positioning statement) with your actual values that we will figure out together by working through this how-to.

Example Positioning Statements

Here are some example positioning statements.

I help boutique fashion brands to build cult-like community followings through emotional storytelling and visual identity design.

I help start-up founders to go from 0 to 1 by applying my proven track-record as an early start-up and founding engineer and decades of experience in software engineering and engineering leadership.

I help postpartum mothers to regain their natural energy levels through hormone-balancing, whole-food meal planning.

I help corporate teams to design deeply engaging hybrid retreats through experiential team-building methodologies.

I help junior and mid-level software engineers to become senior software engineers through applying my experience of having previously become senior software engineer myself, and by using my experience of having managed and hired senior software engineers and having a sense of the qualities of what make for a "good" senior software engineer.
