Personal Branding Positioning Statement How-To

a step-by-step buide on creating a personal brand positioning statement

by Charles Iliya Krempeaux

This How-To is meant to give you a step-by-step guide on how to create a personal branding positioning statement.

For information one what a personal branding positioning statement is see:

  • Personal Branding Positioning Statement

Personal Branding Positioning Statement Template

You want to be able to eventually write a positioning statement about yourself of the form:

I help SPECIFIC_AUDIENCE to ACHIEVE_SPECIFIC_OUTCOME through YOUR_DISTINCTIVE_EXPERTISE_OR_APPROACH_OR_METHOD.

Where “SPECIFIC_AUDIENCE”, “ACHIEVE_SPECIFIC_OUTCOME”, and “YOUR_DISTINCTIVE_EXPERTISE_OR_APPROACH_OR_METHOD” will be replaced (in your positioning statement) with your actual values that we will figure out together by working through this how-to.

Example Personal Branding Positioning Statements

Here are some example positioning statements.

I help non-technical startup founders to go from a rough idea to a fully functional tech company through hands-on 0-to-1 architecture & engineering.

I help grassroots community organizations to secure sustainable donor funding through compelling grant-writing and donor storytelling campaigns.

I help first-time home buyers to purchase their dream properties in competitive markets through proactive off-market sourcing and aggressive negotiation frameworks.

I help overwhelmed couples to plan destination weddings stress-free through curated vendor matchmaking and meticulous day-of execution.
